Board-Certified Adult & Forensic Psychiatrist

Olaya Lizette Solis, M.D.

Compassionate, evidence-based psychiatric care in Columbus, Ohio.

Dr. Solis is a board-certified adult and forensic psychiatrist based in Columbus, Ohio. Her goal is simple: help patients living with mental illness function at their personal best. She believes the most effective care blends medication management, psychotherapy, and lifestyle changes to support patients through difficult times.

Having practiced in both hospital and outpatient settings, Dr. Solis brings the experience to meet patients wherever they are in their illness — and to walk with them toward a better quality of life.

Conditions Treated

  • Mood Disorders
  • Anxiety Disorders
  • Post-traumatic Stress Disorder
  • Psychotic Disorders
  • Personality Disorders
