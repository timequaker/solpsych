Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Dr. Solis take insurance?

Dr. Solis does not take insurance directly. Upon request, she will provide documentation that you can submit to your insurance provider for reimbursement as an out-of-network provider.

What kinds of appointments are available?

New patients are scheduled for a one-hour initial evaluation. Follow-up visits are generally thirty minutes. Dr. Solis' approach combines therapy and medication management at each visit, with the frequency of appointments tailored to each patient's needs and preferences.

How much are the fees for appointments?

Please contact Dr. Solis directly to discuss fees.

Does Dr. Solis conduct evaluations through telehealth visits?

Yes! Dr. Solis understands that patients have busy schedules and that commuting to appointments adds time and stress to the day. Visits are available through doxy.me, a HIPAA-compliant telehealth platform.
